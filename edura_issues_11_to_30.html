The require_role decorator stub was created in issue #04 but left empty. This issue finalises it and implements all user_service endpoints. The decorator is the single most-imported utility across all 11 services — it must be correct and well-tested before any other service builds on top of it. The decorator must work in two ways: as a FastAPI Depends() injection (for route-level protection) and as a standalone function decorator (for internal service calls).
